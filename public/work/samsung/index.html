Overview

At Samsung R&D, I built bootloader and data-security modules across Samsung chipsets, with contributions to ML-based privacy tools and 5G configuration systems. This was low-level engineering where security, chipset variation, and device behavior all mattered.

It also clarified the kind of work I wanted next: closer to product decisions and the people using what I build. That shift toward broader ownership became a thread through my later roles. Samsung Research’s public security and privacy work offers company context, but does not document my specific contributions.
