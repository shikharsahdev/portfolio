Overview

After Samsung, where I had started to feel stagnant, Shipsy was a big change of pace: my first startup, a fast-growing team, and the chance to take on a much wider range of work. I loved the energy and the exposure. I could move between understanding customer needs, shaping product behavior, and working across the front end and back end. One of the largest clients I supported was DTDC, where I worked on pricing and built a returns-and-settlements product from scratch across payments and the full order lifecycle.

When COVID created an urgent need for safer handovers, I worked with the team to get contactless delivery out in about two weeks—a fast response for a large, established logistics application.

I also contributed to a reusable last-mile tracking effort designed to adapt to different client operations, as well as right-to-left and left-to-right support for regional expansion. The experience connected naturally with what I had been doing since school: building products, learning by making, and taking an idea through to something people could use. Shipsy’s last-mile tracking product update and customer story show the broader product and customer context.

Selected contributions

  2. Returns and settlements from scratch

  3. Contactless delivery in two weeks

Takeaway

Shipsy gave me the pace and breadth I was looking for: real customer problems, room to wear many hats, and the chance to bring the product-building instinct I had developed in school into a fast-growing team.
