I find the product in the problem, shape the experience, and build it through.

I'm Shikhar Sahdev, a product engineer in Bangalore. My work starts with the people using a product: what they need, where the friction is, and what would make the experience feel right. I bring engineering depth to those product problems, alongside an eye for experience and a willingness to own the work from idea through launch.

At Goldcast, I helped shape its Events app into the company's core product, then led it for two to three years. I introduced the Backstage concept for speakers and producers and owned WebRTC live audio/video over the long term. Goldcast's event platform became its primary revenue engine, growing to $20M ARR before Cvent acquired the company in December 2025.

I also helped extend the product beyond live events: Recording Studio for asynchronous video creation, Content Lab's transcript-aware AI Video Editor, and a Generative Page Builder. Explore the Goldcast Events platform and the MarDreamin' customer story.

That work draws on a creative and technical practice I've had since I was young: I care about the details people feel, and I can go deep on the systems that make those details possible. Front-end is one of my strongest craft areas, and I bring it to product work from shaping user flows through launch.

Earlier, I built logistics software at Shipsy, a digital trauma-patient enrollment system used at five government hospitals with ICMR and AIIMS, and security systems at Samsung R&D. Each role taught me to look beyond the feature and understand the job a product needs to do.
