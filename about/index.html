Background
& Journey

From discovering Photoshop at 12 to building products that impact millions — here's how curiosity became a career in product engineering.

At 12–13 years old, I discovered Photoshop and web development. What started as curiosity became a lifelong passion for building things that people love and that solve real problems.

School gave me my first exposure to teamwork and leadership. I served as President of XINO, our school's computer club, organized tech initiatives and hackathons, and coordinated inter-school competitions as President of the IT & Quiz Cabinet. I was also a core member of the school mathematics club.

In college, I explored creative expression through DTU Theatre Society — learning how to bring people together, organize events, and work collaboratively on projects that mattered to our community. I learned the value of balancing technical and creative pursuits.

I enjoy music, football, and art — not because I'm particularly good at them, but because they bring me joy and help me think differently. I play guitar and ukulele, kick a football when I can, and doodle when I need a break from screens.
