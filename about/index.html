Background
& Journey

I've always loved making things: first images and websites, then products used by real people. Today I bring a creative eye, product instinct, and deep engineering craft to the same work.

At 12–13 years old, I discovered Photoshop and web development. I loved both the creative side—making something feel alive—and the technical side—figuring out how to make it work. That combination still drives how I build products: understand the person, imagine a better experience, and make it real.

School gave me my first exposure to teamwork and leadership. I served as President of XINO, our school's computer club, organized tech initiatives and hackathons, and coordinated inter-school competitions as President of the IT & Quiz Cabinet. I was also a core member of the school mathematics club.

In college, I explored creative expression through DTU Theatre Society — learning how to bring people together, organize events, and work collaboratively on projects that mattered to our community. It sharpened my sense of timing, story, and how people experience a moment together.

Outside work, I enjoy music, football, and art. I play guitar and ukulele, kick a football when I can, and doodle when I need a break from screens. Creative time keeps me curious and gives me new ways to look at a product problem.
