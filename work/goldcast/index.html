Overview

I joined Goldcast as its second employee, before there was a product. I helped build the Events app into the company’s core product and main business, then led the app for two to three years. I moved between product direction, design decisions, and hands-on engineering to shape an end-to-end event experience.

I helped define the product’s voice and how the experience worked, from attendee flows and event theming to the private Backstage concept for speakers and producers. I led the team’s work on hybrid-event flows and digital check-in for in-person attendees. As the company grew, I mentored engineers, guided teams, and shaped architecture and product strategy across business lines.

I owned Goldcast’s WebRTC audio/video experience over multiple years. The work combined real-time systems engineering with a product goal: make a live event feel dependable and simple for thousands of attendees, including people joining through restrictive enterprise networks.

I also led teams across major extensions to the platform. The Recording Studio was a substantial, multi-team effort that brought asynchronous video creation into Goldcast. I helped teams deliver Content Lab’s transcript-aware AI Video Editor and its Generative Page Builder, carrying product ideas through design and engineering. Goldcast’s platform became its primary revenue engine, reaching $20M ARR before Cvent acquired the company in December 2025. Goldcast was named to Deloitte’s 2025 Technology Fast 500 and supported events for teams at OpenAI, Canva, Mailchimp, Box, Intercom, LG, and Zuora. Explore the Goldcast Events platform, its MarDreamin’ customer story, and Cvent’s acquisition announcement.

As one example of the product’s customer impact, Goldcast’s published MarDreamin’ story reports that the event team saved more than 600 hours and reduced attendee technical-support requests by 89% after switching to the platform.
